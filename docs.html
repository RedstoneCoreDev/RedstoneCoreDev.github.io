Cytherion Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for the Cytherion Discord Bot Network. Here you will find information about our bots, their features, and our policies.

SentriCordAI

SentriCordAI is an advanced AI-powered moderation bot for Discord. It uses machine learning to detect and manage inappropriate content, spam, and more.
Features:

  • Automatic moderation of messages
  • Customizable filters and actions
  • Real-time alerts and logs
  • Integration with Discord's moderation tools

RedBot

RedBot is a classic moderation and automation bot.
Features:

  • Role management
  • Automated welcome/goodbye messages
  • Custom commands
  • Moderation tools (kick, ban, mute, etc.)

Nextherion

Nextherion connects your Discord server with external services and APIs.

Terms of Use

By using Cytherion and its bots, you agree to the following terms:

  • You must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Do not use the bots for illegal or malicious activities.
  • We reserve the right to update or remove features at any time.
  • Abuse of the bots may result in a ban from our services.
  • Severe violations such as scamming, or even joking about suicide or murder threats, will also result in a ban in order to keep the community safe.

Privacy Policy

I only collect data necessary for the bots to function (e.g., user IDs, messages for moderation).

I do not store any personal information beyond what is required for bot functionality.
