Documentation & Policies ▼

Welcome to the official documentation for the Cytherion Discord Bot Network. Here you will find information about my bots, their features, and my policies.

SentriCordAI

AI-powered moderation bot for Discord. Features include:

  • Automatic moderation of messages
  • Real-time alerts and logs

Terms of Use

  • You must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Do not use the bots for illegal or malicious activities.
  • We reserve the right to update or remove features at any time.
  • Abuse of the bots may result in a ban from our services.
  • Severe violations such as scamming or joking about suicide or murder threats will result in a ban from the server where it happens. Repeated offenses, including on multiple Cytherion-connected servers, may lead to a global ban across all Cytherion servers to protect the community.

Privacy Policy

I only collect data necessary for the bots to function (e.g., user IDs, messages for moderation). Moderation-related messages, such as those involving serious violations, are retained permanently, while other data will be deleted if you are no longer on a server connected to Cytherion.

No personal information is stored beyond what is required for bot functionality.

NOTE: Cytherion is in its Alpha state so not everything works perfectly and your Data may be lost or may not get deleted. No guarantees are made.
